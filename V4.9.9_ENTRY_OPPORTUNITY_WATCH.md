# v4.9.9 Entry Opportunity Watch

Purpose: observe good structural Entry Zones earlier without lowering TRADE thresholds.

Rules:
- Context + Setup must be aligned.
- Direction must match aligned Context + Setup.
- Direction Edge must already meet the pair requirement.
- Score 30-34: OPPORTUNITY only.
- Score 35 to below effective required score: existing CANDIDATE / WAIT.
- Score >= effective required score: normal signal pipeline.
- OPPORTUNITY can never become TRADE directly.

The watch runs the Entry Zone Engine with a shadow direction while the real signal remains NO SIGNAL.
This allows the scanner to record:
- Best Entry
- Last Acceptable
- Worst Entry
- TOO LATE status
before the normal Candidate Gate is reached.

Unchanged:
- TRADE score floors 52 / 54 / 55
- candidate floor 35
- Direction Alignment penalty
- Candle Confirmation
- Signal Strength
- TOO LATE / WORST ENTRY hard blockers
