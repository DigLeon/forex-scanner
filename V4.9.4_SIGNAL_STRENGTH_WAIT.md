# v4.9.4 — Signal Strength WAIT optimization

Goal: reduce over-filtering without allowing weak entries to become TRADE.

New final behavior:
- Signal Strength < 40 -> SKIP
- Signal Strength 40-49 -> WAIT
- Signal Strength 50-64 -> WAIT / CAUTION
- Signal Strength 65-79 -> GOOD / RECOMMENDED WITH CAUTION
- Signal Strength 80+ -> STRONG / RECOMMENDED

Important:
- Score thresholds remain unchanged: default 52, USD/JPY & USD/CAD 54, GBP/* 55.
- Context/setup conflict remains a hard SKIP.
- TOO LATE remains a hard SKIP.
- WORST ENTRY / DO NOT ENTER remains a hard SKIP.
- Weak Score and weak Direction Edge remain hard SKIP.
- Candle Confirmation remains mandatory before TRADE.
- Telegram remains TRADE-only.

The new 40-49 band cannot become TRADE. It is preserved as WAIT so the scanner can re-evaluate it on the next analysis cycle instead of discarding it immediately.
