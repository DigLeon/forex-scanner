# v4.9.4 Session Prefilter

## Goal
Improve the chance that the five expensive full-analysis slots are spent on currently active pairs without lowering the existing signal score thresholds.

## Flow
17 supported pairs -> session ranking Top-8 -> quick prefilter -> up to 5 qualified pairs -> unchanged full MTF/SMC/Entry/Candle Confirmation pipeline.

## API/caching policy
The prefilter intentionally reuses the existing 1-minute, outputsize=1500 local-history bootstrap. The previous scanner needed this history for five pairs. v4.9.4 bootstraps up to eight session candidates, so a cold scan adds at most three history requests. Selected pairs then reuse the same local history in the full scan.

## Prefilter score (selection only)
- 30M context: 30
- 15M setup activity: 25
- 5M momentum: 20
- 5M ATR activity: 15
- session relevance: 10
- 30M/15M directional conflict: -8 selection penalty

Minimum prefilter score: 35. Target: up to five pairs. The prefilter does not create a TRADE signal and does not modify the main signal score.

## Safety
Higher-timeframe aggregates use only fully closed buckets. Existing Score thresholds, pair safety floors, Entry Engine, Candle Confirmation, expiration logic, news filter, and Telegram trade gating are unchanged.


## RC2 tuning after live London test

- Prefilter minimum changed from **35 to 28**.
- Prefilter now behaves primarily as a **ranking layer**.
- It selects viable pairs first, then backfills from the highest-ranked remaining successfully scored pairs until the full-scan target of **5** is reached.
- Data/scoring failures are never backfilled.
- Main trading Score, pair safety floors, Entry Engine, Candle Confirmation and Telegram TRADE gate remain unchanged.
