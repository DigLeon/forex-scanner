# v4.9.5 Candidate Gate

Purpose: preserve strongly directional, context/setup-aligned setups for observation without lowering the final TRADE threshold.

Candidate / WAIT requirements:
- Context and Setup are aligned.
- Candidate direction matches the aligned Context.
- Directional score >= 35.
- Directional score is still below effective TRADE minimum.
- Direction Edge >= the normal pair minimum.
- Candidate direction score > opposite score.

Final TRADE requirements remain unchanged:
- Effective score floor remains 52 / 54 / 55 depending on pair/user setting.
- Candidate-only state is forced to WAIT and can never become TRADE.
- Context conflict remains blocked.
- TOO LATE and WORST ENTRY remain blocked.
- Direction Edge requirement remains.
- Candle Confirmation remains mandatory.
- Signal Strength policy remains from v4.9.4.
- Telegram remains final-TRADE only.

Also corrected signal formation to use effectiveMinScore rather than pair-only minSignalScore, matching the documented max(user, pair safety floor) rule.
