# v4.16 — Signal Performance + Early Telegram

Paper-analysis update.

- Persistent `signal-performance.json` lifecycle events: WAIT, GET_READY, READY, SKIP, TRADE.
- Existing `signal-history.json` now stores Entry Zone, Signal Strength, Candle Confirmation, required score, edge and alignment fields for future effectiveness analysis.
- `GET_READY` Telegram alert is earlier than final TRADE and is deduplicated per symbol/direction/FVG.
- GET_READY requires score + edge thresholds, no context/setup conflict, strength >=45, acceptable-or-better entry, and no TOO LATE/WORST/BAD blocker. Candle confirmation may still be pending.
- Final TRADE Telegram remains unchanged and authoritative.
- `/api/signal-performance` returns lifecycle/conversion statistics.
- `/api/signal-effectiveness` estimates historical win rate from similar completed PAPER signals; it returns insufficient confidence until enough samples exist.
- No WebSocket changes. REST Credit Manager remains in place.
