# Forex Scanner v5.0.5 — Telegram on Expiration

- Telegram alerts are gated by a concrete, future expiration time.
- GET_READY may still exist internally before expiration is available, but Telegram waits.
- Full-scan and Fast Recheck GET_READY payloads now carry expiration fields.
- Fast Recheck can send the notification later when expiration becomes available.
- Final TRADE Telegram is also blocked if expiration is missing or already expired.
- Safety gates (TOO LATE / WORST ENTRY / DO NOT ENTER) are unchanged.
- Auto Scan, Candidate Discovery, Fast Recheck and WebSocket behavior are unchanged.
