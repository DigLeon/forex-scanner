# v5.3.4.1 — Expiration-generation price checkpoints

Base: v5.3.4 Historical Pair Priority. The discarded v5.3.5 GUI candidate fix is NOT included.

For every logged WAIT/TRADE record that has an expiration-generation timestamp, the logger now tracks the market price at:
- +5 minutes
- +10 minutes
- +15 minutes
- +20 minutes
- +30 minutes

Each checkpoint has flat fields for target time, target ms, observed price, observed time, status, approximate flag, and source. Example:
`priceAfter15Minutes`, `priceAfter15MinutesStatus`, `priceAfter15MinutesTargetAt`, etc.

The clock starts from `expirationGeneratedAtMs`, not from signal expiry and not from signal completion.

The observer stays interested in a symbol until all of these checkpoints are completed, even if the main signal is already COMPLETED. Historical 1m candle close at or before target is preferred; otherwise a live/shared sample is used.

Existing +20m field names are preserved for backward compatibility.
