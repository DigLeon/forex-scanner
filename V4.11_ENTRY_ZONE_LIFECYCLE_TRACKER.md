# v4.11 Entry Zone Lifecycle Tracker

Purpose:
Track structural Entry Zones independently from the final market signal.

States:
- NO ZONE
- TRACKING
- APPROACHING
- IN ZONE
- MISSED

Direction priority:
1. Real signal direction
2. Opportunity / PRE-OPPORTUNITY direction
3. Directional 1H/30M Context when there is no Context/Setup conflict

The tracker records:
- direction
- current score / required score
- Context / Setup state
- current price
- Best Entry
- Best Zone Low / High
- Last Acceptable
- Worst Entry
- ATR distances
- zone state and reason

Important:
This module is observational only.
It does NOT lower score thresholds.
It does NOT create TRADE.
It does NOT bypass Candle Confirmation.
It does NOT bypass TOO LATE or WORST ENTRY.

Final thresholds remain:
- default 52
- USD/JPY & USD/CAD 54
- GBP pairs 55
