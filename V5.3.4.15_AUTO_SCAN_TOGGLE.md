# v5.3.4.15 Auto Scan Toggle

- Added persistent AUTO SCAN ON/OFF switch in the GUI.
- OFF cancels the scheduled timer and prevents Candidate Discovery/shared automatic work gated by autoScanEnabled.
- Manual SCAN remains available.
- User preference is saved to runtime-settings.json and survives server restarts.
- Manual /api/scan requests no longer force Auto Scan back on.
