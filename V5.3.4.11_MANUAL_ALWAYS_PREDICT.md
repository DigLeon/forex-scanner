# v5.3.4.11 — Manual ALWAYS PREDICT

Manual Analysis now produces an UP or DOWN research prediction on every completed manual scan.
The existing core Score Engine and Entry/Execution Engine are not modified. The normal engine decision
(TRADE/WAIT/SKIP/etc.) is retained as `executionDecision` / `rawDecision` while the paper logger stores
`predictionDirection`, `predictionScore`, `oppositeScore`, `upScore`, `downScore`, `scoreGap`, and
`predictionConfidence`. Outcomes remain PAPER/RESEARCH only and are checked on the selected 3/5/15m horizon.
