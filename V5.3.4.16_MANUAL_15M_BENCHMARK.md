# v5.3.4.16 — Manual 15m benchmark & delay diagnostics

- In REST-only mode Manual Analysis recommends 15 minutes by default; 3m/5m/15m remain selectable.
- Manual stats are split by FULL / PARTIAL / FALLBACK analysis quality.
- Entry-delay buckets: 0–2s, 2–10s, 10–30s, 30s+.
- 15m is shown as the primary research benchmark.
- Each completed Manual record stores maxFavorableMove (MFE) and maxAdverseMove (MAE), reconstructed from available 1m candle highs/lows plus observed prices.
- Manual row cards synchronize with server history after completion and show the final result instead of staying on “waiting for result update”.
- Added missing `fs` import in server.js so AUTO SCAN ON/OFF persists to runtime-settings.json.
- Core Score Engine, Entry Engine and Auto Scanner scoring logic are unchanged.
