# Forex Scanner v5.0.2 — Current Decision Isolation

- Historical Entry Zone lifecycle no longer emits current `ZONE WATCH` scan decisions.
- Historical zones remain available through Zone History / lifecycle diagnostics.
- Early `OPPORTUNITY` requires its freshly calculated opportunity Entry Zone to be actionable.
- `TOO LATE`, `WORST ENTRY`, and `DO NOT ENTER` opportunity zones cannot become current OPPORTUNITY decisions.
- Current scan response remains deduplicated by symbol: one pair = one final current decision.
- Final TRADE thresholds, GET READY logic, Auto Scan 10 min, Outcome Engine and WebSocket behavior are unchanged.
