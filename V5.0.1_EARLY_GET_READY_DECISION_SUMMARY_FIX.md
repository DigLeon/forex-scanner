# Forex Scanner v5.0.1 — Early GET READY + Unique Decision Summary

## Fix 1 — Early GET READY score tolerance

GET READY is an early PAPER-analysis warning, not a final TRADE decision. It can now trigger up to `GET_READY_SCORE_TOLERANCE` points below the strict pair score threshold (default `2`) when all other early gates remain strong:

- direction edge passes;
- Context/Setup has no conflict;
- Signal Strength is at least 45;
- Entry is not BAD / WORST / DO NOT ENTER / TOO LATE.

The final TRADE gate is unchanged and still requires the normal strict score threshold and confirmation rules.

## Fix 2 — One pair = one scan decision

Lifecycle diagnostics such as ZONE WATCH may be logged during analysis, but `/api/scan` now collapses current decisions by symbol and returns/counts only the last authoritative decision for each pair. This prevents a pair from being counted as both `ZONE WATCH` and `SKIP` in the same scan.

## Small logging fix

`[FAST RECHECK WATCH]` logging was unreachable because it appeared after a `return`. The log is now emitted before returning the watch object.

Auto Scan remains 10 minutes. WebSocket behavior is unchanged.
