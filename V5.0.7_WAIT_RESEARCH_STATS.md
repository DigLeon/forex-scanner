# v5.0.7 WAIT Research Statistics

- Real TRADE statistics remain isolated and unchanged.
- WAIT setups are additionally persisted when `score > 60` (strictly greater than 60).
- WAIT records use the same 3/5/10/15/30m outcome tracker as TRADE records.
- WAIT and TRADE duplicate guards are independent, so a WAIT record cannot block a later TRADE.
- One WAIT research record is kept per lifecycle setupId + decision where setupId is available.
- `/api/signal-stats` exposes `waitAbove60` separately from `trade`.
- WAIT is only logged when a valid expiration horizon and price are available.
