# v5.3.3 Research Mode — GUI Sync + 10 Pairs + Score 50 + Price +20m

Changes in this build:

- GUI now polls `/api/latest-scan` every 5 seconds and automatically applies the latest completed FULL Auto Scan. Manual clicking is no longer required to see Auto Scan decisions.
- GUI keeps TRADE / WAIT / READY / CANDIDATE / OPPORTUNITY / PRE-OPPORTUNITY / ZONE WATCH rows. Research candidate rows can remain visible even when they are below the display score threshold; SKIP stays hidden.
- Full market scan limit increased from 5 to 10 pairs (`FULL_SCAN_PAIR_LIMIT=10`). Candidate Discovery limit is also 10 by default.
- Pair safety minimum signal score is now 50 for all configured pairs. Edge and entry-quality rules are unchanged.
- WAIT research logger now records WAIT setups with score >= 50 (only when an expiration exists and the price is valid). TRADE statistics remain separate.
- Added the 20-minute research horizon: 3m / 5m / 10m / 15m / 20m / 30m.
- Every new logged WAIT or TRADE record contains a dedicated checkpoint measured from the moment its expiration timestamp is generated:
  - `expirationGeneratedAt`
  - `expirationGeneratedAtMs`
  - `priceAfter20MinutesTargetAt`
  - `priceAfter20MinutesTargetAtMs`
  - `priceAfter20Minutes`
  - `priceAfter20MinutesObservedAt`
  - `priceAfter20MinutesStatus`
  - `priceAfter20MinutesApproximate`
  - `priceAfter20MinutesSource`
- The +20m price continues to be tracked even if the main expiration has already completed. This also applies to WAIT records.
- `getPendingSignalSymbols()` keeps symbols alive until the dedicated +20m checkpoint is captured.
- Statistics now expose `waitAtOrAbove50`. The legacy `waitAbove60` slice remains for backward compatibility.

Unchanged:
- Telegram delivery threshold remains strictly score > 60.
- Entry Engine, candle confirmation, expiration selection, stale-data protection, API-economy guards, and Telegram dedupe behavior are not loosened.
