# Forex Scanner v4.13 — Adaptive Prefilter + FVG Birth Tracker

## Combined base
This build starts from v4.12.3 and keeps its persistent structural Entry Zone history, tracker-placement fix, PRE/Opportunity/Candidate stages, score diagnostics, candle confirmation and existing decision pipeline.

## Adaptive Session Prefilter
Flow: 17 pairs -> Session Ranking Top 8 -> lightweight Prefilter -> dynamic Top 5 -> existing full scanner pipeline.

Prefilter components:
- 30M Context: 0-30
- 15M Setup Activity: 0-25
- 5M Momentum: 0-20
- Volatility / ATR: 0-15
- Session Relevance: 0-10

Reference minimum is 28. If fewer than five pass, the scanner fills the remaining slots with the strongest successfully evaluated Top-8 pairs.

All 5M/15M/30M candles are aggregated locally from the existing closed 1M history. No separate timeframe REST requests are added.

### Entry Opportunity ranking bonus
A fresh 5M/15M FVG close to current price can add up to 8 ranking points inside the prefilter only. This bonus cannot change final directional score, pair safety floors, Entry Engine, Entry Zone, Candle Confirmation, or TOO LATE protection.

## FVG Birth Tracker
The scanner now observes structural FVGs before a final signal exists on 1M, 3M, 5M, 15M, 30M and 1H.

Stable identity: symbol + direction + timeframe + formation datetime.

Runtime files:
- fvg-birth-state.json
- fvg-birth-history.jsonl

Events:
- FVG_BORN
- FVG_ANALYSIS_UPDATE

API:
- GET /api/fvg-births?symbol=EUR/USD&limit=100
- GET /api/fvg-birth-history?symbol=EUR/USD&limit=100
- GET /api/fvg-birth-history?zoneId=<ZONE_ID>&limit=100
- POST /api/fvg-birth-history/clear

## Existing safety/decision gates unchanged
- PRE 25
- Opportunity 30
- Candidate 35
- final floors 52 / 54 / 55
- MTF conflict checks
- Candle Confirmation
- Entry Zone / Entry Engine
- TOO LATE / WORST ENTRY hard blocks
- Signal Strength gating

The new prefilter and birth tracker are observational/routing layers; they do not bypass the existing final decision pipeline.
