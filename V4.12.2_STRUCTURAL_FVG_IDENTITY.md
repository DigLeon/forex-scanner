Primary Zone ID identity: symbol + direction + timeframe + FVG formation datetime.

The same FVG keeps the same deterministic Zone ID even when Best Entry or zone boundaries drift. A different formation candle creates a new ID even if price ranges overlap. Legacy v4.12/v4.12.1 records may fuzzy-match once and are then upgraded with formationDatetime. formationIndex is diagnostic only.
